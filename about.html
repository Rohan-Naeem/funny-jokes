About Funny Jokes

Funny Jokes is a simple blog with one job: give you a quick, clean laugh. The site started on 17 November 2026 with 100 original jokes in 10 categories — short jokes, dad jokes, knock-knock jokes, one-liners, jokes for kids, animal jokes, food jokes, work jokes, school jokes, and clean classic jokes.

New jokes are added slowly, 2 to 4 at a time, so the list grows without turning into a rushed dump of recycled jokes. Every joke on this site is written to be clean and family-friendly.
