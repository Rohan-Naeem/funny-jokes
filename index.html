What Makes a Joke Funny?

A good joke usually has three things: a surprise, a little wordplay, and a situation people recognise. The setup leads you one way, and the punchline turns it around. Short jokes work best when every word counts, and a joke lands better when it fits the people listening — a school joke for classmates, a work joke for coworkers, a kids joke for children.

How to Tell a Joke

Keep the setup short. Pause for a beat before the punchline. Use a natural voice and a straight face. Read the room — pick a joke that fits the moment. And do not explain the punchline afterwards; if it lands, the laugh will tell you.
